# AeroForge engineering benchmarks

## Scope and evidence rules

This ledger verifies the M4.2–M4.4 mission, constraint, and preliminary-sizing pipeline at model version 1.0.0. Expected analytical values were calculated independently from the equations below and stored in [`src/validation/fixtures/m4-5-benchmarks.json`](../src/validation/fixtures/m4-5-benchmarks.json); they are not snapshots generated by the production solver. Regression coverage is in [`src/validation/m4_5_benchmarks.test.ts`](../src/validation/m4_5_benchmarks.test.ts).

Passing these cases verifies implementation of the declared low-fidelity equations. It does not validate takeoff, a complete mission, propulsion maps, battery current/voltage behavior, aircraft-specific empty-mass correlations, flightworthiness, or certification.

### Governing equations

- Troposphere: `T=T0-Lh`, `p=p0(T/T0)^[g/(R L)]`, `rho=p/(R T)`, `a=sqrt(gamma R T)`.
- Stall: `(W/S)max=0.5 rho0 Vs,max² CLmax`.
- Cruise: `q=0.5 rho V²`, `CL=(W/S)/q`, `k=1/(pi e AR)`, `CD=CD0+k CL²`, `D/W=q CD/(W/S)`, and `Pshaft/W=V(D/W)/eta_p`.
- Climb: `(Pshaft/W)climb=(Pshaft/W)cruise+ROC/eta_p`.
- Service ceiling: `CLmp=sqrt(3 CD0/k)`, `Vmp=sqrt[2(W/S)/(rho CLmp)]`, then add `0.508/eta_p` W/N for the 100 ft/min criterion.
- Geometry and installed power: `S=W/(W/S)` and `Pinstalled=(P/W)installed W`.
- Cruise shaft energy: `E=Pshaft,cruise R/V`.
- Electric storage: `mb=E/[eb(1-r)]`; fuel storage: `mf=E cb/(1-r)`.
- Mass closure: `m=mfixed+mpayload+menergy(m)`. The independent closed form is `m=(mfixed+mpayload)/(1-beta)`, where `beta` is the propulsion-specific energy mass fraction.
- Reserve: `Etotal=E/(1-r)` and `Ereserve=Etotal-E`.

NASA’s case is a retrofit study with climb, cruise, descent, VFR reserve, balked-landing power, state of charge, voltage, and current effects. The source does not publish the complete empty-mass, aerodynamic, segment-power, and reserve inputs needed to reproduce it with AeroForge’s M4.4 equations. The 16% battery fraction is therefore not used as a tunable target. NASA’s work specifically shows that simple specific-energy models can be optimistic when end-of-mission power is current- or voltage-limited.

## Defects and discrepancies

- No production-equation defect was found in M4.2–M4.4.
- The first stall fixture used more density digits than the cited `1.2250 kg/m³`; the fixture was corrected to source precision. Production code was unchanged.
- The direct sizing algebra was extracted to a pure `calculateSizedGeometryAndPower` helper and reused by M4.4 so the published-case regression tests the same production path. This is a traceability refactor, not an equation change.
- M4.3 curve interpolation accounts for the small power-loading differences above and remains well inside the declared 0.05% tolerance.

## M4.6 readiness decision

**Not ready for unqualified automatic aircraft generation.** The analytical pipeline is verified within its declared equations, and the NASA case provides a credible direct wing/power sanity check. However, no published case with all required empty-mass, aerodynamic, propulsion, reserve, and mission inputs was available to validate the complete M4.4 closure without invented values. Takeoff remains mandatory and unevaluated, and the electric model does not check pack power, voltage, current, state of charge, or segment reserves.

M4.6 may proceed only as an explicitly provisional candidate generator that keeps results separate from authoritative geometry, requires user review/apply, retains `indeterminate` mission status, and carries every M4.4 limitation forward. Automatic authoritative application should remain blocked until a matched full-pipeline reference case and takeoff/mission-energy coverage are approved.
